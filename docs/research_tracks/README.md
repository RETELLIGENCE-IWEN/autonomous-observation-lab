# Research Tracks

Research tracks turn the broader vision into falsifiable questions, explicit hypotheses, baselines, experiments, and decision criteria.

The overarching conceptual architecture is [Think–Dream–Look (TDL)](../vision/think_dream_look.md). Individual tracks deliberately test narrower mechanisms rather than requiring a single monolithic TDL system from the start.

## Initial tracks

- [Dream-to-Look](dream_to_look/README.md) — validates the **Dream → Look** mechanism through object-centric latent imagination.
- [Hypothesis-Driven Active Observation](hypothesis_driven_observation/README.md) — studies belief gaps, competing hypotheses, and decision-relevant evidence seeking; closely related to the **Think** side of TDL.
- [Epistemic Distillation](epistemic_distillation/README.md) — transfers rich belief and uncertainty structure into deployable limited-observation agents; a cross-cutting enabling technique for TDL.
